Market Integrity Policy:
Identifies communications suggesting market manipulation, collusion, front running, or any coordinated activity that may unfairly influence securities prices or trading behavior.

Insider Information (MNPI) Policy:
Identifies communications involving material non-public information (MNPI), including its sharing, receipt, misuse, or trading based on such information, including tipper-tippee scenarios.

Personal Trading Compliance Policy:
Monitors communications indicating violations of personal trading rules, including trading restricted securities, failure to pre-clear trades, or misuse of confidential information for personal gain.

Information Security & Data Leakage Policy:
Detects unauthorized sharing of sensitive client, financial, proprietary, or confidential information, including documents, screenshots, or structured data leaving approved systems.

Records Retention & Communication Archiving Policy:
Identifies attempts to avoid or violate recordkeeping requirements, including deletion, suppression of communications, or use of unapproved or non-archived channels.

Communication Channel Compliance Policy:
Identifies movement of business communications to unapproved platforms (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram) or use of unauthorized devices or tools for business activity.

Conflicts of Interest & Outside Activities Policy:
Detects undisclosed outside business activities, personal conflicts, or situations where employee interests may influence professional responsibilities.

Gifts, Entertainment & Improper Benefits Policy:
Identifies communications involving gifts, hospitality, or other benefits from vendors or clients that may require disclosure or violate policy thresholds.

Regulatory Conduct & Investigation Integrity Policy:
Detects attempts to evade, obstruct, or mislead regulatory inquiries, audits, internal investigations, or surveillance processes.

Misconduct & Misrepresentation Policy:
Identifies false, misleading, or deceptive communications related to financial products, services, performance, or regulatory disclosures.

Client Confidentiality Policy:
Detects unauthorized disclosure of client identities, holdings, transactions, or other sensitive client relationship information.

Bribery & Corruption Policy:
Identifies communications suggesting bribery, kickbacks, improper inducements, or unethical payments involving clients, vendors, or intermediaries.

Conduct & Workplace Ethics (HR) Policy:
Identifies discriminatory, harassing, abusive, or offensive language including sexism, racism, religious or gender-based discrimination.

Cybersecurity & Access Control Policy:
Identifies attempts to compromise systems, credentials, or accounts, as well as failure to follow proper reporting procedures for security incidents.

Technology Use & AI Governance Policy:
Identifies use of artificial intelligence, bots, automation tools, or technology intended to bypass or evade controls or surveillance mechanisms.
